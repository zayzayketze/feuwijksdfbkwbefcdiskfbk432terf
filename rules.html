Our Standards

Clear rules. Fair enforcement.

Our rules exist to protect the community and ensure everyone can enjoy VoidHaven. We enforce them consistently and fairly for all players.

Core Rules

What we expect from every player.

01

Respect Everyone

Be courteous, kind, and fair to all players and staff. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or bullying of any kind. Treat others as you'd want to be treated.

02

No Griefing

Protect builds and player property without exception. Destroying or modifying another player's work is strictly prohibited. Respect land claims and boundaries.

03

Fair Progression

No cheating, hacks, or exploits that break the intended experience. X-ray, aimbots, speed mods, and duplication exploits are forbidden. Play fairly.

04

Build With Purpose

Leave the world better than you found it. No random building or spam. Create with intention and take pride in your creations. Respect the aesthetic of shared spaces.

05

No Spamming

Keep chat clean and readable. No spam, flooding, or advertising. Respect others' time and space to communicate. This applies to Discord and in-game chat.

06

Follow Staff Decisions

Staff decisions are final. If you disagree with a ruling, appeal respectfully through proper channels (Discord). Don't argue with staff in public chat.

07

No PvP Without Consent

PvP is only allowed in designated areas and with mutual agreement. Don't attack players in survival zones without permission. Griefing during PvP is still forbidden.

08

Keep It Family-Friendly

VoidHaven is for all ages. Avoid explicit language, sexual content, and mature themes. Keep conversations appropriate and inclusive for everyone.

Consequences

We enforce our rules fairly.

⚠️ Warning

First-time minor offenses receive a warning. We'll explain what went wrong and how to fix it.

⏸️ Temporary Mute

Chat violations result in brief muting periods. Multiple offenses lead to longer mutes.

🔄 Rollback

Griefing or destruction is immediately reversed. The offender may face additional penalties.

⛔ Ban

Serious or repeated violations result in temporary or permanent bans. Appeals are considered.

Transparency

How we handle disputes.

Appeals Process

If you believe you've been punished unfairly, you can appeal through Discord's #appeals channel. Include your username, what happened, and why you think the decision was wrong. A neutral staff member will review your case within 48 hours.

Evidence & Logging

We keep detailed logs of all player actions, chat, and staff decisions. When we make a call, it's backed by evidence. Transparency protects everyone—players and staff alike.

Staff Code of Conduct

Staff members are held to the same standards as players, plus additional expectations. They cannot abuse power, and they report to leadership. If staff misconduct occurs, report it immediately to the Admin team.

Community Input

Players can suggest rule changes or improvements. We regularly survey the community and consider feedback. Our rules exist to serve the community—not the other way around.
