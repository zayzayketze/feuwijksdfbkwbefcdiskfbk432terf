About the Server

A premium survival experience with purpose.

Built for players who value creativity, cooperation, and long-term progression, VoidHaven SMP blends immersive survival mechanics with a tightly knit player base. Whether you are crafting your dream base, competing for the richest empire, or forming lasting alliances, this is a server designed for the long haul.

We emphasize fair play, polished community standards, and memorable events that keep the world alive. Every build tells a story, and every player has a place in the Haven.

Discorde allowed? +

We allow client-side optimization mods (Sodium, Iris, etc.) and cosmetic mods only. No cheating mods, X-ray, or gameplay-altering exploits. Full list available in the Discord.

We recommend players be 13+, as discord will ban your account if younger, though exceptions are made for younger players with active adult supervision. Our community is family-friendly and respectful.

We run long-term worlds. Server resets happen annually or when consensus is reached by the community. Your builds are always celebrated before any reset.
