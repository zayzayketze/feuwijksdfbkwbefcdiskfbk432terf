Getting Started

Application & Access

Applications are open on our Discord server. Join using the link on this website, navigate to #applications, and fill out the form. We review all submissions within 48 hours and prioritize passionate builders and team players. You'll receive a response via DM once your application is reviewed.

Tell us about yourself and your Minecraft experience. Share your playstyle, what you're excited to build, and why you want to join VoidHaven. Be genuine! We're looking for players who share our community values, not just experienced builders.

VoidHaven SMP is completely free. There are no pay-to-win features, no server pass, and no donations required. We believe in keeping the playing field fair for everyone.

Once you're approved, you'll get the server address and credentials in Discord. We keep the IP private to prevent unauthorized access. The address is mc.voidhaven.net:25565

Technical

Mods, Versions & Specs

We currently run Minecraft 1.20.2. Updates are scheduled regularly to support new versions. Check our Discord #announcements for update schedules and migration info.

Yes! We allow client-side optimization mods like Sodium, Iris, Lithium, and Phosphor. Cosmetic mods (like custom capes or Better Animals Plus) are also fine. No cheating mods, X-ray, aimbots, or gameplay-altering exploits. We provide a full approved mod list in Discord.

We support Fabric, Forge, and Quilt loaders. Mix and match mods from different loaders as you like. Just remember: only use mods from our approved list to avoid getting kicked or banned.

VoidHaven runs on high-performance hardware with 99.9% uptime. We actively monitor performance and optimize regularly. If you experience lag, it's likely a client-side issue—make sure you're using optimization mods and have sufficient RAM allocated to Minecraft.

Community

Age, Playstyle & Interaction

We recommend players be 13 years old (Discord's minimum), though exceptions are made for younger players with active parent/guardian supervision. Our community is family-friendly and respectful.

VoidHaven welcomes all skill levels! We have veteran builders and newcomers alike. Our community is incredibly helpful—people will offer resources, guidance, and collaboration. You'll never feel left behind.

PvP is allowed in designated arenas only. Survival areas are PvP-free to protect builders. Never engage in PvP without consent from the other player. Breaking this rule results in punishment.

Both playstyles are totally welcome. Build alone, with a small group, or join larger factions. Many players are independent builders with friends. You choose how you want to play.

Server Operation

Resets, Wipes & Long-Term Plans

We run long-term worlds designed to last years, not months. Server resets happen annually or only when consensus is reached by the community. Your builds are always celebrated before any reset—we document everything.

Your builds and claims are protected indefinitely. We don't wipe inactive players. If you take a break and come back months (or years) later, everything will be there.

Applications open quarterly. Watch #announcements in Discord for open applications. We look for players with excellent community standing, mature judgment, and genuine desire to help. Experience with Minecraft administration is a plus.

Head to Discord #appeals and submit a formal appeal. Include your username, what happened, and why you believe the decision was unfair. A neutral staff member reviews within 48 hours. Be respectful—rude appeals will be denied.
