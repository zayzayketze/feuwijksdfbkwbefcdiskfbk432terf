Before You Join

What we value most.

✅ We Welcome

  • Players of all skill levels and ages (13+)
  • Builders, traders, explorers, and PvPers
  • Casual and hardcore players alike
  • People ready to respect the community
  • Those excited about long-term gameplay

⛔ We Don't Accept

  • Players with a history of griefing or toxicity
  • Those planning to use cheats or exploits
  • Accounts under 13 years old (without parental permission)
  • Anyone unwilling to follow our community rules
  • Duplicate/alt accounts of banned players
